Parcel Out — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 24 October 2026

1. Who is responsible
Parcel Out (Android package com.parcelout.game) is provided by CRYBLADE STUDIO. For privacy questions, corrections or data requests, contact crybladestudio@gmail.com. This policy covers Parcel Out, not other games from the developer.

2. Local play and your choices
You can play without online services. Core gameplay does not require Google sign-in, advertising consent or cloud sync. On first launch we explain online services and ask for an age band, without requesting a date of birth. The age band and online-mode preference are stored in the app's private device storage, separately from game progress. They are not included in cloud saves. For players aged 16–17, a teen-treatment signal is supplied to Google's advertising SDK. We do not send a date of birth.
Parcel Out is intended for people aged 13 and older. As an additional safeguard, players who select an age below 16, or do not specify an age, use local play without app-initiated advertising, consent or Play Games requests. Selecting a younger age keeps that installation restricted; it cannot be changed to an unrestricted age through the game's settings.

3. Game data stored on the device
The app stores level results, scores, stars, coins, unlocked cosmetic styles, favorites, settings, tutorial progress, daily challenges, reward limits and unfinished games, including lives and undo history. Local backup files may contain earlier versions of this information. This information is used to operate the game and restore progress. We do not upload local backups to a developer-operated server.

4. Optional Google Play Games and cloud saves
If you enable online services and connect Google Play Games, Google authenticates your gaming profile. The game receives a game-specific player identifier and display name. The player identifier is used to associate a cloud save with the correct gaming profile. A cloud save includes the game progress and settings described above, its revision and a save timestamp. It does not include your Google password, email address, payment information, local age-band preference or advertising consent choices.
Cloud data is processed through Google Play Games Saved Games. Local versions and replaced cloud versions can be kept in backup files on the device to reduce accidental loss. Disconnecting synchronization stops future game-initiated synchronization but does not delete existing local or cloud data. Google's own SDKs may also process account information, service analytics and diagnostics as described in Google's privacy documentation.

5. Advertising and consent
When online advertising is allowed, Parcel Out uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for banners, interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. Google may process IP addresses (including an approximate location inferred from IP), device and advertising identifiers, ad/app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, service operation and analytics. We do not request precise device location for Parcel Out.
Before the game initializes advertising or loads ads, it requests updated privacy information through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google displays a consent or privacy message when required for the player's region and configuration. Advertising requests are allowed only when UMP reports that they are permitted. Refusing personalization is not the same as switching off all advertising; Google may permit limited or non-personalized ads. If the privacy check fails, the game does not start new ad requests until a successful check. Age-specific protections are configured for players aged 16–17, and ad content is capped at Google's T rating.
You can reopen advertising choices from Settings → Privacy and data → Advertising choices / retry, where Google makes a privacy form available. You can also choose Play without online services to stop new app-initiated ad requests and disconnect cloud sync while retaining local play. Technical testing builds can use Google's demo ad units; this does not mean that SDK network processing is absent.

6. Third parties and disclosure
Google processes information through AdMob, UMP and Play Games. Their services can involve Google affiliates and advertising technology providers listed in the relevant privacy message. Advertising-related transfers may qualify as sharing or selling under some local privacy laws even though CRYBLADE STUDIO does not directly sell player information for money. Use the available privacy controls and regional opt-out options to exercise applicable choices.
We have not integrated session replay, email marketing, paid subscriptions, a separate developer-operated player account system or a developer-operated gameplay analytics backend into this version. Google's SDK diagnostics, analytics and advertising measurement are described above. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to handle your request; do not send passwords or authentication codes.

7. Legal grounds and regional controls
Where applicable, we seek consent for processing that requires it. Other processing may be necessary to provide requested functionality, protect the services against fraud, meet legal obligations or pursue legitimate interests where permitted by law. The exact grounds and available rights depend on the processing and your location. UMP provides relevant Google advertising privacy choices; it does not replace this policy or your rights to contact us.

8. Retention and deletion
Local progress, preferences and backup files remain until you clear the app's data or otherwise remove them. To clear them on Android, open Settings → Apps → Parcel Out → Storage → Clear data (names can vary). This resets local progress and privacy preferences. It does not delete the separate Google cloud copy or Google account data.
To delete cloud progress, close Parcel Out, open your Google Play Games profile settings, select the option to delete profile/game data, and delete the individual game Parcel Out. Do not delete your entire Play Games profile unless you intend to remove data for other games too. Google explains its available deletion controls at https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646 . Clearing local data and deleting cloud data are separate actions. Reconnecting with a remaining local or cloud copy may restore that copy, so remove both if you want a complete game-progress reset.
Google retains technical, account and advertising information under its own retention rules and available account/privacy controls. Contact us about information you have sent directly to us or to obtain help with a request. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. We cannot sign in to your Google account or delete Google's independent records on your behalf.

9. Your rights
Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. Contact crybladestudio@gmail.com. We may need proportionate information to verify and handle a request. You may also contact your competent data-protection authority where applicable. Turning off online features does not remove rights in already processed information.

10. Security and international processing
Google SDK and cloud communications use encrypted transport. Device-local save files are stored in the application's private storage; we do not claim that local JSON backup files are separately encrypted. No system can guarantee absolute security. Google and hosting providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable terms and safeguards.
