Parcel Out

A little order. A lot of possibilities.

A parcel puzzle game for Android, developed by CRYBLADE STUDIO. Follow arrows to clear a route, collect three parcels of the same color and keep the dispatch tray from filling up.

Three ways to play

Delivery puzzles. Explore 960 levels across eight chapters, with frozen parcels, colored seals and other parcel mechanics. Endless conveyor. Collect matching parcels from a moving conveyor and try to beat your record with three lives. Postal inspection. Rotate deliveries and compare their packaging, weight, labels and other details with a consignment document.

Play offline or connect optional services

Core gameplay works offline. Progress is saved on your device, and no separate developer account is required. Eligible players who enable online features can connect Google Play Games to synchronize progress across Android devices. Google authenticates the gaming profile; Parcel Out uses a game-specific player identifier, display name and Saved Games data to provide synchronization. The game does not receive your Google password. Advertising and Google services are described in our privacy policy.

Player choices

Parcel Out is intended for ages 13 and older. In the current game, players below 16, or players who do not select an age band, use local play without app-initiated advertising or Google Play Games requests. You can play locally without enabling online services.
